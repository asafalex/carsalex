• Source: your uploaded Excel file ("all" sheet, all platforms combined), September 2026 snapshot — this is a static file, not a live database connection.
• Owner names are matched case-insensitively (e.g. "Tom" and "tom" are combined).
• Rows with an unknown (#N/A) commission rate are treated as ₪0 commission in this summary, but are still counted as segments.
• "Segments" counts matching rows (app × platform × product × country combinations) in the source data.
• The "Company" toggle shows what each amount is worth to the company: employee commission is stated to be 2% of total company value, so the toggle divides by 2% (×50) and converts from ₪ to $ at a fixed rate of $1 = ₪3.0637 (both figures provided by the user).
