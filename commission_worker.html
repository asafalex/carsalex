Notes

• Source: your uploaded Excel file, combining the android, iOS, ots+sams and RT sheets (the "all" sheet is intentionally excluded from this page's totals — it's only used for the comparison column).
• Each sheet's trailing subtotal row (blank owner, a SUBTOTAL/SUM formula) was excluded — it is not a real segment.
• Owner names are matched case-insensitively (e.g. "Tom" and "tom" are combined).
• Rows with an unknown (#N/A) commission rate are treated as ₪0 commission in this summary, but are still counted as segments.
• "all" sheet (₪) is that owner's total commission from the "all" sheet (same source as the Commission dashboard). Match compares the two: a mismatch means the "all" sheet and the individual platform sheets disagree on that owner's commission for the same underlying segments — worth checking which one has the correct rate.
• FTD Match compares total FTD count the same way (sum from the 4 platform sheets vs. the "all" sheet) — any mismatch here points to a counting difference rather than a commission-rate difference.
• The "Company" toggle and "Extra" % work the same as on the Commission page (2% of company value, $1 = ₪3.0637).
